How to play

  • Both fleets have 5 vessels on a 10 × 10 sea: Argo (5 cells), War Trireme (4), Bireme (3), Merchant Galley (3), Fishing Skiff (2).
  • Take turns firing at the enemy sea. Each turn you fire either one arrow or one catapult — never both.
  • An arrow strikes a single cell. A catapult strikes 5 cells in a plus shape: the target cell and the four cells directly above, below, left and right of it.
  • You and your rival each get 2 catapult volleys for the whole battle, so spend them wisely.
  • Sink all 5 enemy vessels to win.
  • Pick a difficulty above: Easy fires nearly at random, Medium hunts down any vessel it hits, Hard searches methodically and times its catapults.

Muster your fleet

Drag a vessel to move it, click it to turn it, or let the harbour master arrange the fleet for you.
